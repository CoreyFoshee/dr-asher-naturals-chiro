Physical Therapy

Physical Therapy

Dr. Asher completed his certification in physical therapy, via the National Board of Chiropractic Examiners.

Dr. Asher uses his knowledge of physical therapy to educate his patients on specific therapeutic exercises and/or stretches to further assist with patient recovery time.

He also helps to educate his patients on how their daily activities may be affecting their condition. Certain motions that patients do on a regular basis can begin to accumulate day after day and lead to pain. As Dr. Asher corrects your spine, he also teaches patients how to avoid activities that contribute to their condition.

Feel, move, and live better with Dr. Asher Natural Chiropractic.
