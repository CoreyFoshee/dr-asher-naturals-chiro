Adjustments

Dr Asher is an expert when it comes to the diagnosis and treatment of spinal and extremities disorders. To identify the cause of pain, every patient undergoes radiographic, orthopedic, and neurological examinations. Once identified, we naturally work with your body to correct subluxations via the chiropractic adjustment. These adjustments restore the proper position of misaligned bones and vertebrae, reducing pressure on surrounding nerves. As a result, once this improper nerve pressure is relieved, your body no longer signals pain in that area. Experience the difference with our personalized chiropractic care.

Accu-Spina IDD Therapy

Dr. Asher works with one of the most prestigious non surgical groups of America right now, the “Disc Centers of America.” This group focuses on treating the source of bulging, herniated, and extruding discs, of the neck and lumbar spine. The reason bulging and herniated discs cause so much pain, burning, tingling, numbness, or weakness in the neck, low back, arms, or legs is because the cartilage between your vertebrae, the intervertebral disc, is being compressed into the sensitive nerves exiting your spinal cord. With the Accu-Spina table, we work to provide the opposite force that damaged the disc to begin with. This FDA approved technology works to remove the compression from the disc by stretching the disc with targeted oscillation. This treatment differentiates itself from other tables on the market by providing IDD (Intervertebral Disc Dynamics) therapy that creates negative disc pressure that is computer targeted specifically to your damaged area.

Cold Laser Class IV

Cold laser therapy has been approved by the FDA for the use in the United States. Cold laser therapy assists in the regeneration of tissues by increasing the availability of nutrients to the area, it accelerates soft tissue and bone repair, improves nerve regeneration and function at the cellular level, increases cell metabolism, enzyme function, collagen production, and assists in bringing in more oxygen to the area. All of these increase the rate of healing to the area, which also help to decrease pain.

Conditions commonly treated with Cold laser therapy include but not limited to – muscle pain, arthritis pain, knee pain, shoulder pain, wrist pain, foot pain, gout, tendonitis, headaches, and some neuropathies to name a few.

Treatment for muscle spasms or “Pulled” muscles

Wondering how chiropractic care assists with reducing muscle spasms or pulled muscles? Every muscle in your body connects to your bones through what’s known as a tendon. Once a chiropractor corrects bone misalignments, tendons and muscles begin to relax. Dr. Asher also utilizes an instrument known as the “Erchonia percussor.” This instrument allows Dr. Asher to release deeper muscle groups compared to traditional methods.

Electrical Stimulation and Heat Therapy

In addition to chiropractic care, Dr. Asher offers advanced muscle therapies using “low volt galvanic electrical stimulation.” This modality functions as a specialized “massage,” effectively reducing muscle spasms and alleviating pain. We also provide heat therapy through hydrocollator packs, to further assist in relieving muscle tension.

Graston Technique

Graston techniqe uses a set of smooth stainless steel insturments to provide deeper penetration that helps to break up muscle adhesions that restrict normal joint movement. This abnormal joint movement creates improper pressure on the surrounding nerves, which leads to pain. These stainless steel intsturments are designed with unique angles that allow the practitioner to scrape and mobilize the tissue. This technique is used for multiple conditions, such as tendonitis, plantar fascitis, muscle strains, and sometimes helps to decrease scar tissue.
